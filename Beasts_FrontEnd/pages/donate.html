100% volunteer-run · 501(c)(3)

Keep PNEC ready.

PNEC operates entirely on donations and volunteer hours. Every dollar funds drills, kits, training, and outreach across all 14 Poway neighborhoods.

📚

Training materials

CERT manuals, first-aid cards, and preparedness guides for every coordinator and workshop participant.

🎒

CERT equipment

Hard hats, safety vests, first-aid kits, and search lights for response teams.

🏘️

Community outreach

Neighborhood fliers, multilingual materials, and door-to-door coordinator campaigns.

📻

Radio infrastructure

PACT amateur radios, repeater maintenance, and equipment for emergency communications.
